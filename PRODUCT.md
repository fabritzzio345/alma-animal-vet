# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: buildless static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for a fast, accessible landing page that can be published without a framework or runtime dependency.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: pet guardians and families looking for a trustworthy clinic where they can solve routine, preventive, grooming, surgical, emergency, and shopping needs in one place.

## Product Purpose

Inferred from the brief: present a complete veterinary clinic, help visitors quickly understand its care coverage, and convert high-intent visitors into appointment or emergency-contact actions.

## Positioning

Inferred: one continuous care relationship across medicine, surgery, prevention, grooming, and everyday pet supplies, instead of sending guardians to disconnected providers.

## Operating Context

Inferred: visitors may arrive worried, in a hurry, or comparing options on a phone. The site must separate urgent care from routine booking, make service coverage scannable, and show that grooming and retail support medical care rather than distract from it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Spanish-language marketing landing page.
- Explain general consultation, vaccination and prevention, diagnostics, surgery, hospitalization, dentistry, dermatology, senior care, grooming and bathing, and an illustrative pet-products shop.
- Provide a demonstrative appointment form and clear emergency contact path. No backend appointment system or payment processing is claimed.
- The clinic name, staff, contact information, opening hours, prices, testimonials, and statistics are illustrative and must not be represented as verified real-world facts.

## Brand Commitments

Inferred and user-delegated for this concept: the fictional name is **Alma Animal**. Voice is calm, competent, direct, and affectionate without childish pet clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

No real clinic facts, certifications, staff profiles, testimonials, prices, or customer data were supplied. The page must label its contact and commercial content as demonstrative where a visitor could mistake it for real information.

## Product Principles

- Make urgent next steps unmistakable.
- Explain care through concrete services, not vague promises.
- Treat medical trust and everyday warmth as equally important.
- Keep the mobile path fast, legible, and thumb-friendly.
- Never fabricate clinical credentials or commercial proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inferred baseline: semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive labels, and responsive behavior from small mobile screens through desktop.
