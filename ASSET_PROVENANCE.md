# Asset provenance

The four shipping raster assets were generated specifically for the Alma Animal concept with OpenAI image generation. Their prompts were derived from the approved composition and are retained under `.impeccable/build/plate-inputs/` in the working archive.

The production files under `dist/assets/plates/` are optimized JPEG derivatives. Exact paths, SHA-256 hashes, source records, and transformations are recorded in `dist/assets/provenance.json`.

No third-party stock photograph, logo, testimonial, professional identity, or clinic credential is represented as real.
